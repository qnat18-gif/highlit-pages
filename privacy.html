Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

1. Who we are

Highlit ("we", "the app") is an educational YouTube channel and the private publishing tool that supports it. Contact: gnat1698@gmail.com.

2. Information the app accesses

The app connects to the YouTube account of the channel owner only, after the owner gives permission through Google sign-in. With that permission it can:

  • upload videos, thumbnails and video details (title, description, tags, schedule) to the owner's channel;
  • read comments posted on the owner's channel.

3. How information is used

Channel access is used only to publish the owner's videos and to read comments so lesson requests can be considered. Comment text is used in the moment to choose topics and is not published, sold or shared.

4. Storage and sharing

We do not collect personal information from visitors of this website. We do not sell, rent or share user data with third parties. The access token that lets the app work is stored as an encrypted secret in the private automation environment and is never made public.

5. YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Revoking access

The channel owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After access is removed the app can no longer reach the channel.

7. Children

This website and the app are not directed at children and do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: gnat1698@gmail.com.
