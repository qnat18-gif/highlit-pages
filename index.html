About this application

Highlit Publisher is a private tool that prepares educational videos for the Highlit YouTube channel and uploads them to the channel owner's own YouTube account. It also reads comments on the channel so the owner can see which lessons viewers ask for.

What the app does

  • Creates educational videos (math, science, history, exam preparation) for US high school students.
  • Uploads and schedules those videos on the channel owner's YouTube channel.
  • Reads public comments on the channel to find lesson requests.

Who uses it

The app is used only by the owner of the Highlit channel. It is not offered to the public and does not collect data from visitors of this website.
